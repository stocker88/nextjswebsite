## About this policy

This policy covers the Stocks To Buy Now AI website and mobile app, including accounts, research tools, community features and subscriptions. For privacy, account or billing questions, contact [support@stockstobuynow.ai](mailto:support@stockstobuynow.ai).

Stocks To Buy Now AI provides investment research and education, including human analysis, data-driven tools and AI-assisted features. It is not an automated trading service. Investment outcomes are not guaranteed; options can lose their entire premium.

## Information we handle

- **Account information:** email address, account identifier, username, profile details and information supplied by your chosen sign-in provider. We do not receive your Google, Apple or other sign-in provider password.
- **Content and preferences:** posts, comments, messages, uploaded images, saved items, watchlists, portfolio entries, questions submitted to AI features and support requests.
- **Membership information:** purchased plan, payment provider, transaction references, subscription status, renewal and expiry information used to manage access. Payment details are handled by the relevant payment provider.
- **Device and usage information:** browser and device identifiers, sessions, sign-in attempts, diagnostic information, pages and features used, notification tokens, and campaign or referral attribution. These help maintain access, understand signup and purchase journeys, and troubleshoot errors.

Camera, photo-library and notification access depend on device permissions. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

## How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, deliver research and community features, answer questions, manage subscriptions and device access, send notifications, respond to support requests, investigate misuse and improve the service. Usage and attribution information helps us understand how people discover and use the website and app.

Where applicable, processing relies on providing the requested service, legitimate interests such as security and service improvement, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. You can contact support about your choices or objections.

## Cookies, local storage and analytics

The website uses cookies and local storage for account sessions, preferences, browser recognition and attribution. A first-party browser identifier can persist for up to 180 days to distinguish repeat attempts from new visitors; it does not prove that visits are from the same person. Google Analytics may process usage and device information. Clearing browser storage resets local identifiers and may sign you out or remove preferences. Device notification preferences can be changed in the app or operating system.

## Service providers and sharing

We use Google Cloud services for account and application infrastructure, and Google Analytics for usage measurement. Apple, Google Play, Stripe and RevenueCat process relevant payment or subscription information depending on where you purchased. AI features may send the questions and context needed to answer them to external AI service providers. Do not submit sensitive personal or financial credentials in prompts or public posts.

Content you publish in community areas may be visible to other users and shared outside the service. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer, with notice where required. Providers may process data in countries outside your own, subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

## Subscriptions, cancellation and refunds

Check your receipt or **Settings → Manage Membership** to identify your plan and billing provider. Use the same store account that made the purchase. Removing the app or deleting your service account does not itself cancel a store subscription.

### Apple App Store purchases

Manage or cancel Apple-billed subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. Request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). Apple determines eligibility under its policies and applicable consumer law; eligibility can vary by country or region. See [Apple’s refund guidance](https://support.apple.com/118223) and [cancellation instructions](https://support.apple.com/118428).

### Google Play purchases

Manage or cancel Google-billed subscriptions in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Cancellation normally stops renewal while access continues for the period already paid. Refunds are a separate request and depend on Google Play policies, the purchase and applicable law. Follow [Google Play’s refund process](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574897?hl=en); Google directs users to contact the developer for refund requests made more than 48 hours after purchase. See [subscription cancellation instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en).

### Website purchases

Website payments use Stripe checkout and billing management. Use your membership management options or contact [support@stockstobuynow.ai](mailto:support@stockstobuynow.ai) with your account email and transaction reference for billing or refund assistance. Do not send full card numbers or security codes.

### Money-back requests

Cancellation and a refund are different actions. This page does not create an automatic money-back guarantee. Any specific offer shown at purchase, the applicable store or purchase terms, and your statutory consumer rights govern eligibility. Contact support if a purchase was not delivered or access is not working.

## Access, correction and account deletion

You can edit profile information in the app’s settings and contact support to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify account ownership.

To delete your account in the current app:

1. Open your profile and **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy & Legal**, select **EULA Terms of Use** on iOS or **Terms of Use** on Android.
3. Scroll to **Delete Account** and complete the confirmation and authentication steps.

If you cannot access the app, email [support@stockstobuynow.ai](mailto:support@stockstobuynow.ai) from your account email. Cancel any recurring subscription separately through its billing provider.

## Retention and security

Account deletion removes the active account and associated records handled by the deletion flow. It does not guarantee immediate erasure of every record: the current system retains a deleted-account record, and support, transaction, security or backup records may remain. Contact support for a personal-data erasure request and information about retained records. Retention should be limited to the purposes described here and applicable legal requirements.

We use access controls and managed infrastructure to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Contact support to report a suspected account or data-security issue.

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact support so we can investigate and take appropriate action. External websites and payment or sign-in providers have their own policies; review those policies when using their services.
