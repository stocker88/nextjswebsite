Mover links in the app and website

Use the shared moverThreadCoverage projection. Existing recent research has priority: matching session/direction mover report, then company research, then recap/general coverage. Keep links available under the existing access rules.

For positive returns only, when no research link is available, show Signal linking to the oldest isAIAdvisor=true post with an exact symbol tag in the selected calendar-age window (inclusive):
1M: 1–3 months; 3M: 3–6 months; 6M: 6–12 months; 1Y: 1–2 years; 3Y: 3–6 years; 5Y: 5–10 years.
YTD: prior January 1 through current January 1, enabled after 28 days of the current year.
Clamp month ends in UTC. Break timestamp ties by ascending post ID. Deleted, muted, private and option-result posts are excluded. No stock-price prediction or trade entry is inferred. Public metadata contains the title, date and routing identity, not the paid article body.

Live-feed headlines are a separate fallback only for 1D, pre-market and after-hours. Do not request or display them for 1W or longer, even if previously cached. Existing research and options-result links take precedence over a live headline. Both platforms require an exact explicit ticker tag and a publication in the past seven days.
