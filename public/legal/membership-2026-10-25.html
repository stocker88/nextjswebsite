Your Pro membership

These terms cover subscriptions purchased on the Stocks To Buy Now AI website through Stripe. App Store and Google Play purchases follow the relevant store’s billing and refund rules.

Monthly Pro costs US$30 billed every month. Annual Pro costs US$240 billed every year, equivalent to US$20 per month. An annual purchase is a single yearly payment, not monthly instalments. Stripe shows the final currency, conversion and any applicable taxes before you confirm payment.

Pro access is available in the app after successful payment. Sign in with the same account you used to purchase. Live Pro web is planned to launch on December 1, 2026, with a live video demo; the demo time and viewing link will be announced. Research and educational tools do not guarantee investment results.

Automatic renewal and failed payments

Your subscription renews automatically for the selected billing period at the price shown at purchase, unless you cancel renewal before the next renewal date. Any future price change will be communicated before it applies.

For a previously paid Stripe subscription, failed renewal payments have a maximum 48-hour access grace period from the original payment deadline. Retries do not restart that period. Pro access is suspended if the renewal remains unpaid after that deadline, even if Stripe continues trying to recover payment. Access can resume after successful payment is verified. An unpaid first purchase does not receive Pro access.

You can update your card through Membership → Manage subscription & payment card. Stripe may send billing and failed-payment emails with a secure payment-update link. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription.

Cancellation, restoration and plan changes

Cancel renewal through Membership → Manage subscription & payment card → Cancel membership. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the period already paid for. You can also contact support@stockstobuynow.ai for help.

Before an eligible cancelled subscription expires, you can restore its renewal. The confirmation shows the renewal date and recurring price; restoring renewal does not charge for a new period immediately.

Eligible monthly members can switch to annual billing with credit for unused time. Annual-to-monthly changes take effect at the next annual renewal. Stripe displays the charge and effective date before confirmation. Special, discounted or older plans may require support to change.

An eligible monthly member may receive a one-time offer of 20% off the next monthly renewal during cancellation. The offer expires one hour after it is first presented, cannot be restarted, applies once per account and does not apply to annual plans. The regular monthly price resumes afterwards. You can cancel without accepting an offer.

Refunds

We do not automatically refund unused time or provide prorated refunds when you cancel a subscription. Cancellation stops renewal and keeps access available through the paid period.

This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights, including any applicable withdrawal, cancellation or refund rights. Contact support@stockstobuynow.ai with the account email and payment reference if you believe a charge is incorrect, duplicate or otherwise refundable. We assess those requests individually. Never email your full card number or security code.

For Apple App Store or Google Play purchases, request billing support or a refund through the store that processed the payment.

Billing privacy and purchase records

Our existing Privacy Policy and EULA continue to apply. This notice explains additional records used for website billing. Stripe collects and stores your billing name, billing address, card and transaction details. The website displays only your card brand, last four digits and expiry date, and does not store the full card number or security code in Firebase.

We link the Stripe customer and subscription to your authenticated account ID so your membership is recognised in the app and on the website. We retain a purchase-consent record containing your account ID, the accepted policy version and content fingerprint, the selected plan and price, server-recorded acceptance time, and the related Stripe checkout and payment references. Stripe’s checkout consent result is retained when it is available.

We use these records to operate subscriptions, respond to billing questions, prevent misuse and provide relevant evidence in a payment dispute. Relevant records may be shared with Stripe, payment networks and financial institutions handling that dispute. Records are kept as needed for these purposes and applicable accounting and legal requirements. Contact support@stockstobuynow.ai for privacy or account requests.
